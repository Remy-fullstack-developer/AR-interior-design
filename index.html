Welcome to RoomVista

Build your room layout in three simple steps:

  1. Start the camera and allow browser camera permission.
  2. Choose furniture from the library and press Add.
  3. Arrange your room by dragging, resizing, rotating or removing objects.

True 3D AR requires a supported phone/browser and an HTTPS connection (or localhost). If AR is unavailable, the camera preview mode still lets you arrange furniture.
